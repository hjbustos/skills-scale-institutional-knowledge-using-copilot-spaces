# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategy. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria, perform comprehensive testing, and validate that quality standards are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and QA strategy aligned with project scope
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before features move to Done
- Identify and document quality issues with severity and impact
- Collaborate with Developers on test automation and coverage
- Prepare smoke tests and release validation steps
- Track quality metrics and defect trends

### Goals
- Ensure software quality and reliability before production release
- Reduce production incidents through comprehensive testing
- Enable fast, confident releases through repeatable test processes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partner on test automation strategy, code coverage targets, and defect triage
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria interpretation and test coverage priorities
- **With Project Managers**: Provide quality status updates and release readiness assessments

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup participation for quality updates
- Test plan review with Product and Project Managers
- Issue reporting and defect triage meetings
- Pre-release validation and sign-off

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, approve project scope and budgets, and provide strategic guidance. They are the primary decision-makers for go/no-go and prioritization calls.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and scope
- Provide business context and success metrics definition
- Make prioritization decisions during planning and execution
- Review and approve release readiness
- Escalate risks and blockers to executive leadership if needed
- Communicate project outcomes to their organizations

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business strategy
- Maximize return on investment
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and confidence

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on outcome definition and strategic alignment
- **With Project Managers**: Receive status updates and approve scope changes
- **With Developers**: Provide context on business requirements and success criteria

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Weekly or milestone-based status updates
- Risk and blocker escalation
- Release announcements

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic oversight, cross-project alignment, and escalation support. They serve as the senior authority on product direction and help resolve conflicts between projects or teams.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve project one-pagers during initiation
- Align multiple projects toward unified product vision
- Serve as escalation point for product and prioritization decisions
- Mentor Product Managers and ensure consistency in approach
- Review major architectural or design decisions
- Communicate product strategy to leadership and partners

### Goals
- Maintain coherent product strategy across initiatives
- Unblock teams through timely decision-making
- Ensure alignment between individual projects and broader business goals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Provide strategic guidance and escalation resolution
- **With Project Managers**: Align roadmap and prioritization across projects
- **With Developers**: Review technical feasibility of strategic initiatives
- **With Stakeholders**: Coordinate business priorities across portfolios

### Typical Communication
- Weekly PM sync participation
- Project initiation review and approval
- Escalation meetings for conflicts or major decisions
- Quarterly strategic reviews

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, technical strategy, and design review. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in design and architecture decisions
- Review technical proposals for feasibility and risks
- Identify technical dependencies and integration points
- Mentor Developers on best practices and patterns
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs
- Contribute to technical risk identification and mitigation

### Goals
- Ensure code quality, scalability, and maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable efficient knowledge sharing across the team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Mentor on technical standards, design patterns, and best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and implementation trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review sessions
- Architecture discussions during planning
- Code review and mentorship
- Technical risk escalation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach (Optional)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies and practices. They remove impediments to delivery and help teams improve their processes and collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Track and report on team velocity and health metrics
- Help resolve team conflicts and communication challenges
- Promote continuous improvement and learning culture

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Improve team collaboration and psychological safety
- Enable rapid iteration and delivery of value

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Support removal of impediments and facilitate collaboration
- **With Product Managers**: Ensure clarity of priorities and acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate on timelines and commitment management
- **With all roles**: Foster communication and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Leading agile ceremonies (daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives)
- One-on-one coaching and support
- Metrics reporting on team health and velocity
- Continuous improvement discussions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When managing projects, refer to these personas to clarify roles, responsibilities, and communication patterns.
